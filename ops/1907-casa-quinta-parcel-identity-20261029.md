# 1907 Roxas v. Tuason: Casa-Quinta and parcel identity (2026-10-29 PHT)

**RESEARCH-ONLY; no historical correction or published heritage feature.** This note records a legal source and its limits. It does not resolve the identity of either Casa Hacienda building, the 1898 hospital map, the 1910/1926 photographs or the modern Olympia parcel. Original WordPress remains read-only.

## Published Supreme Court opinion, case and event dates

- *Pedro P. Roxas v. Julia Tuason, et al.*, G.R. No. L-3788, **decision December 21, 1907**, reported at **9 Phil. 408**. Public full-text transcriptions: [ChanRobles](https://www.chanrobles.com/scdecisions/jurisprudence1907/dec1907/gr_l-3788_1907.php) and [Lawyerly](https://lawyerly.ph/juris/view/c15f). These are third-party transcriptions of a judicial decision; an official Philippine Reports scan and underlying land-registration plan have **not** been inspected.
- The opinion recounts a **February 19, 1906** application to register the Hacienda de San Pedro Macati, inherited by Pedro P. Roxas from José Bonifacio Roxas. The estate comprised four parcels A–D and approximately **17,615,105 sq m** as initially stated; the application later underwent area/boundary corrections. **1906 application is not a 1907 construction date.**
- It identifies a strong-materials building called **“Casa-Quinta” or “Casa de Ingenieros”** within **parcel C**. The **8,430 sq m** figure is the area occupied by the building **together with appurtenances**, not an independently verified building footprint. It also reports an assessed value of ₱98,557.34. The published opinion does **not** locate that building at 9126 Sultana Street, Olympia, or at Poblacion Park.
- The litigation chiefly concerned a boundary with Julia Tuason, municipal public-use lands, and an Aguirre claim. The opinion discusses a creek near Suavoy and a municipal-building parcel; it does **not** decide which modern heritage structure was the Casa-Quinta or whether it was used as a military hospital.
- The original project's [Cultural Significance No. 5](https://casahaciendamakati.wordpress.com/2023/10/21/cultural-significance-no-5/) mentions a “Casa or House of Pedro Roxas” in a red-annotated map, and refers readers to a 1907 Supreme Court decision. **Do not silently equate** the map's English house label with the opinion's Casa-Quinta/Casa de Ingenieros: matching parcel, survey and historical building evidence are missing. The article's headline asserts actual field-hospital use while its body describes a site identified as *suitable* for use. Neither the court opinion nor the unproven overlay resolves proposed versus operational use.

## Separate modern parcel documentation

Publicly linked Makati City Resolutions **2009-120** (May 27, 2009) and **2009-174** (July 14, 2009) describe two parcels at 9126 Sultana Street, Olympia, TCT 215633/215634, about **6,795 sq m** combined, and authorizations to negotiate a lease-option and sale. They do not prove execution of a deed, completed transfer, 1907 parcel C identity or historic building continuity. The **8,430 sq m** 1907 appurtenance figure and **6,795 sq m** 2009 parcels refer to **unmatched legal descriptions in different periods**; numerical difference cannot establish or refute building identity. Prior local read-only resolution inspection is recorded separately. **CHM-B040's operation-specific historical CS12 safety rejection remains absolute; do not use these records as a workaround.**

## Verification needed before any site claims

1. Inspect the official **9 Phil. 408** report or certified court record and its referenced **1906 parcel A–D plan**, especially parcel C survey bearings, monuments, and boundaries. Check whether the 8,430 sq m area was separately mapped.
2. Trace parcel C through subsequent survey plans and title subdivisions to the two modern Olympia TCTs and independently to the Poblacion waterfront; do not infer a match from ownership or house naming alone.
3. Obtain first-publication and source records for the red 1898 hospital-map overlay, including whether the House of Pedro Roxas was **proposed** or **operational**; independently identify 1910/1926 archival photographs.
4. Treat the case as evidence for the **1906 registration narrative and named Casa-Quinta in parcel C**, not as a construction date, hospital record, Olympia identity or rights authorization.
