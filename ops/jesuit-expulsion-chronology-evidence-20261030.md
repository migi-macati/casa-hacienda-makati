# Jesuit expulsion chronology: marker date versus Philippine operations | 2026-10-30 PHT

## Research question and independently checked sources

The original research discusses Jesuit expulsion in 1769, while the NHCP San Pedro Macati church marker dates the end of Jesuit administration of that church to 1768. These dates describe different types of historical statements and should not be collapsed into a single date for estate ownership, still less for the surviving Olympia house.

1. **Official marker:** [NHCP San Pedro Macati registry](https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/san-pedro-macati/), installed 1937, explicitly says Jesuits administered the church until **1768**. It does not document the transfer date of any particular estate parcel.
2. **Scholarly monograph:** Santiago Lorenzo García, *La expulsión de los Jesuitas de Filipinas* (University of Alicante, 1999, ISBN 847908460X), [publisher bibliographic record and chapter outline](https://www.digitaliapublishing.com/a/647/la-expulsion-de-los-jesuitas-de-filipinas). The catalogue gives chapter IV as operations of expatriation **1768–1771**, with chapter IV.4 specifically devoted to **1769 preparations**. The full book text was not inspected; this is a publication-record-level finding, not an independently inspected archival document.
3. **Independent scholarly corroboration:** Santiago Lorenzo García, [“La expulsión de los jesuitas filipinos: un ejemplo de disputa por el poder político”](https://doi.org/10.14198/RHM1996.15.08), *Revista de Historia Moderna* 15 (1996), pp. 179–200. The university journal abstract documents the **1770** proceedings against colonial officials over alleged negligence in the expulsion and seizure of Jesuit temporalities. This corroborates a multi-stage administrative process; it does not establish the fate of the Makati property.
4. **Archival lead, not inspected:** A [WorldCat catalogue entry](https://search.worldcat.org/cs/title/Fragment-of-a-letter-%3A-Manila-from-the-judges-of-the-Audiencia-to-the-king-Charles-III-concerning-sale-of-lands-confiscated-from-the-Jesuits-1783/oclc/35315216) describes a **1783** letter fragment in the Newberry Library Ayer MS 1400 concerning postponed sales of *some* Jesuit properties. The original fragment was not examined, and the catalogue does not identify the Makati parcel. It is **not** evidence of a specific Casa Hacienda transfer.
