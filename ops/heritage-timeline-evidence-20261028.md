# First evidence-led timeline segment — source assessment (2026-10-28)

This is a limited **published homepage feature**, not acceptance of the full Casa Hacienda timeline, and not proof that the surviving Olympia building stood in 1607, 1608, 1620 or 1768.

## Reviewed sources and dating distinctions

| Date shown | Narrow assertion supported | Source and date of record | Exclusions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1607 | Donation of the Buenavista site and endowment of Jesuit novitiate attributed to Captain Pedro de Brito | NHCP [San Pedro Macati registry](https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/san-pedro-macati/); inscription of a **1937** historical marker | No original deed examined; no proof of the Olympia building's construction |
| 1608 | Encomienda grant to Captain Pedro de Brito | NHCP [Makati registry](https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/makati/); inscription of a **1991** historical marker | A different legal act; do not silently reconcile with the 1607 site donation |
| 1620 | Construction of San Pedro Macati church by the Jesuits | NHCP 1937 marker (https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/san-pedro-macati/) | Church date, not Casa Hacienda construction date |
| 1768 | Last year of Jesuit administration of the church recorded by the 1937 marker | NHCP 1937 marker (https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/san-pedro-macati/) | No precise Hacienda transfer or ownership conclusion |

Both official marker records were checked against the current NHCP registry on 2026-10-28. They are authoritative evidence **of the inscriptions** but are later summaries, not the underlying seventeenth-century deeds. The 1607 and 1608 records concern distinct acts. The first segment deliberately avoids claims of physical continuity between the Poblacion church/novitiate precinct and the surviving Olympia structure.

## Research still awaiting primary/source review

The published WordPress material proposes a 1773 archiepiscopal visitation, a 1775 map, 1910 and 1926 photographs, and a 2018 fire. These are not included in the verified timeline segment until the original record, dating, provenance and interpretation are checked. Salvador P. Escoto's scholarly study of the 1773–1775 visitation ([publisher](https://archium.ateneo.edu/phstudies/vol58/iss1/7/)) is a lead, not independently checked original visitation manuscript. Its article date must not be mistaken for the date of a visit.

No images, PDF derivatives, third-party text quotations, WordPress writes, private drafts, media rights decisions, or public-launch changes are part of this change. The historical CS12 correction restriction CHM-B040 is unrelated and remains untouched.
