## Filipinas Heritage Library photographs: access is not permission

Primary institutional sources checked 2026-10-30 PHT:
- Reproduction terms: https://www.filipinaslibrary.org.ph/terms-and-conditions/
- Visitor/reproduction access: https://www.filipinaslibrary.org.ph/booking/
- Retrato archive: https://www.filipinaslibrary.org.ph/retrato/the-retrato-photo-archive/
- Original public project cites two FHL catalogue URLs: https://www.filipinaslibrary.org.ph/biblio/18057/ (1910 claim) and https://www.filipinaslibrary.org.ph/biblio/18040/ (1926 claim). The two item pages were **not successfully retrieved** in this check; original item captions, subject identity, accession/barcode, exact provenance, and licensing remain unverified. An original WordPress article's citation is evidence of what that article claims, not independent verification of the catalogue record.

FHL's published **Retrato Photo Reproduction** terms distinguish collection access, image-owner authorization, copyright-holder permission and publication-specific usage. They state that a reproduction is for the specified use, owner authorization and applicable fees are required, permission from the copyright holder remains the requester's responsibility, and some contracts are non-transferable. FHL reserves the right to refuse reproduction. Its booking page lists an ordinary day pass at **PHP 300** and Retrato reproduction at **PHP 200 per image** (rates subject to change); these are published access/service prices, **not** a grant of permission and not necessarily a complete licensing cost. No booking, fee, account or request was made.

The Retrato page says its ordering system has technical issues and directs photo requests to the library with photo ID/barcode or page link. **Safe next step:** obtain original catalogue metadata and ownership/rightsholder information for the two identified records before asserting photograph-to-parcel continuity or commissioning new reproductions. Do not assume a watermarked image, a public link, or presence on the original WordPress site grants a fresh right to reproduce it.
