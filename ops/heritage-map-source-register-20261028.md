# Source register: 1775 Manila plan and early Casa Hacienda evidence
Checked 2026-10-28 PHT. Research-only: **not a new timeline milestone or confirmation of the Olympia structure**. Source date, map subject and depicted building identity must remain separate.

## Confirmed original map catalog identification
- Holding institution: **British Library**, Western Manuscripts, Additional Manuscripts, F. Bauzà collection (Add MS 17556–17676).
- **Exact shelfmark: Add MS 17641 C**. Record ID `040-002027896`; archival identifier `ark:/81055/vdc_100000001516.0x000068`.
- British Library's catalog describes a **1775** paper-roll manuscript plan of Manila and its surrounding coast and bay, titled *Plano de los contornos, porcion de costa y bahia adyacentes á la ciudad y plaza de Manila, capital de las Yslas Philipinas*, attributed in the catalog to **Diono. Kelley** (also cited as Dionisio Kelly).
- Primary institutional catalog: https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-002027896 . This establishes the existence, title, author attribution, repository and year **of the map**, not a date of construction of any house.
- The catalog lists **no digitised content** for this item. The actual map sheet, legend, scale, place labels and depiction of the J.B. Roxas/Olympia location have **not** been independently inspected. A specific Casa Hacienda site or building **cannot yet be inferred**.
- **Do not confuse Add MS 17641 C with Add MS 17641 D**: https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-002027897 is a different plan by **Gregorio Clavero dated 1800**, despite a cross-reference to Kelly's 1775 plan.

Published Cultural Significance No. 1 identifies a 1775 plan by Dionisio Kelly as evidence of the location of Casa Hacienda: https://casahaciendamakati.wordpress.com/2023/08/12/cultural-significance-no-1/ . Its source link leads to a limited-access Google Books view of a later publication. The British Library catalog now resolves the original **map's identity** but **does not validate the visual interpretation** in the article. Treat the original article's “earliest cartographic evidence of the Casa Hacienda” claim as an interpretation awaiting examination of Add MS 17641 C, not a verified event or feature.
