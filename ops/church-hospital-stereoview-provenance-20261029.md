**Identity hierarchy:** The three records independently establish that institutions catalogue a stereoview under closely matching **San Pedro Macati church/hospital titles**, with Underwood publishing/collection associations. They do **not** independently establish three distinct photographic exposures, a date of a hospital event, an 1898 Spanish military hospital at the Casa/House of Pedro Roxas, or any connection to 9126 Sultana Street. The Ortigas **U250** series designation and Smithsonian **4601 / RSN 26465** are distinct local numbering systems; do not equate them without visual and verso comparison. The Library of Congress catalogue title is **from the item**, not independent proof that a hospital operated at the photographed church.

## Source chronology and rights boundaries

- **1898:** The project's [published Cultural Significance No. 5](https://casahaciendamakati.wordpress.com/2023/10/21/cultural-significance-no-5/) describes a Spanish military field-hospital map naming the Casa/House of Pedro Roxas. The map's original repository, legend, sheet date, georeferencing, proposed-versus-actual-use status, and building identity have not been verified. Preserve the original published article without silently correcting it.
- **c1899:** LOC publication/catalogue date for a **church** stereograph. Ortigas finding aid indexes **©1899** for U250. Neither is an independently verified exposure date.
- **1895–1921:** Smithsonian **collection-level** date range, not a date for the interpositive. The Smithsonian catalogue's “1900–1910” genre tag is also not a securely dated exposure.
- **Reuse:** No image downloaded, copied, embedded, OCR'd, altered, or published. LOC “no known restrictions” is not a blanket project authorization. Smithsonian conditions require checking; Ortigas access and rights require confirmation. The four private drafts, 82 migrated media and noindex are unchanged.
