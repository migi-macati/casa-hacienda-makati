The Casa Hacienda of Makati

9126 Sultana St. cor. J.B. Roxas St., Barangay Olympia, Makati City

NAME

For most of its 400 years of existence, this building had been known to locals and to those who frequently pass Makati via the Pasig River by these names:

Casa Hacienda

Casa Quinta

Casa Principal

Casa de Campo con Camarin

AGE

We do not know exactly when the Casa Hacienda Makati was built, but sources show that it has been standing in its current place for 100 or more than 250 years.

What we are sure of is that a casa hacienda has been built by the Jesuits or their administrators by the time they were expelled from the Spanish Colonies in 1769. When the Marquis of Villamediana acquired the hacienda from the Spanish Crown in 1795, he had to repair the "excelente casa" that came with the hacienda.

CURRENT STATE

The remains of the building is still standing. Sadly, a 4-storey city warehouse has been constructed at the frontyard while an animal care facility is currently under construction within the compound.

Unfortunately, the site is inaccessible at the moment due to its ruinous state.
